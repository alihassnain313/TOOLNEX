Privacy Policy

This page explains how TOOLNEX handles information when you use our website and tools.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TOOLNEX. We respect your privacy and aim to keep the use of our website simple, transparent and secure.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit TOOLNEX, how that information may be used and the choices available to you.

2. Information You Provide

Some parts of TOOLNEX may allow you to provide information directly, such as when you contact us or submit feedback.

This information may include details such as your name, email address and the content of your message.

3. Files and Tool Inputs

Certain TOOLNEX tools may allow you to enter text or select files from your device.

The way information is processed depends on the specific tool and how that tool is implemented. You should avoid submitting sensitive, confidential or private information unless the tool clearly explains how it is handled.

4. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit a website, basic technical information may potentially be collected by the website infrastructure or service providers.

This may include information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited and general usage information.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TOOLNEX or third-party services used by the website may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, analytics, security or advertising purposes.

Cookie behavior may change as website features and services are added or updated.

6. Advertising

TOOLNEX may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers.

These providers may use technologies such as cookies or similar identifiers to deliver, measure or personalize advertisements according to their own policies and applicable rules.

7. Analytics

TOOLNEX may use analytics services to understand general website usage, identify technical problems and improve the user experience.

Analytics providers may process technical and usage information according to their own privacy policies.

8. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • operate and maintain TOOLNEX;
  • improve website features and tools;
  • respond to questions and support requests;
  • detect abuse, security problems or technical issues;
  • understand general website performance;
  • support advertising and analytics where applicable.

9. Third-Party Services

TOOLNEX may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, advertising, security, communication or other website functions.

These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect information associated with the website.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security, and you should use caution when sharing sensitive information online.

11. Children's Privacy

TOOLNEX is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

12. External Links

TOOLNEX may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TOOLNEX introduces new features, services or changes to its operation.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact TOOLNEX through the contact page.
